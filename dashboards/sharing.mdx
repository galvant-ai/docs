Share a live dashboard so teammates can open it, explore its results, and ask Dash questions. You choose who can view it. To send an Excel report instead, see [Export and email reports](/dashboards/reports).

## Share with selected people or your company

Open a dashboard you own and click **Share**. Choose its audience:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Selected people">
    Choose **Specific people**. Use **Add people** to find a teammate by name or email, then select them. Check the names under **People with access** and click **Save changes**.

    <Info>
      **GIF placeholder: SHARE-01**

      Add one teammate under Specific people, check People with access, and save the change. Use a publishable name and email address.
    </Info>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Your company">
    Choose **Everyone**, then click **Save changes**. The access list shows **Everyone in the company**. Company users with Dashboards access can open it.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Only you">
    Choose **Private**, then click **Save changes**. The dashboard is available only to you, its owner.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Existing data access permissions still apply. The selected audience takes effect when you save it.

## Send a dashboard link

After saving the audience, reopen **Share** and click **Copy link**. Send the link to the people who should use the dashboard. They sign in to Galvant to open it.

<Info>
  **Screenshot placeholder: SHARE-02**

  Show Copy link beside a saved Everyone in the company audience. Keep the dashboard title visible.
</Info>

<Note>
  Copying a link does not add someone to the audience. **Copy link** opens the dashboard with its saved variable defaults. To share your currently selected period or other inputs, copy the address from your browser instead.
</Note>

See [current selections and saved defaults](/dashboards/variables#current-selections-and-saved-defaults) for how those views differ.

## What viewers can do

Viewers see **Shared dashboard · Read only**. They can explore the dashboard without changing its saved setup.

| Action | Owner | Viewer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explore variables and table rows | Yes | Yes |
| Refresh, download Excel, or ask Dash questions | Yes | Yes |
| Change saved cards, variables, or layout | Yes | Make an owned copy first |
| Change access, rename, or delete the dashboard | Yes | No |

To adapt a shared dashboard, click **Duplicate and edit**. This creates a separate copy you own.

## Change or remove access

Reopen **Share** on a dashboard you own. Under **Specific people**, remove a person with their remove control, or choose a different audience. Click **Save changes** to apply it.

For example, change **Everyone** to **Specific people** when only a smaller group should retain access, or choose **Private** to keep it to yourself.

Changing live-dashboard access does not retrieve files already downloaded or emailed. Review any [email schedules](/dashboards/reports#change-or-stop-a-schedule) when the report's audience changes.

<Accordion title="A teammate cannot open the dashboard">
  Check the saved audience first. The person must also have an eligible company account with Dashboards access. If their entry shows **Access unavailable**, ask your Galvant administrator to check their account access. See [dashboard access troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting#a-dashboard-or-card-is-unavailable).
</Accordion>
