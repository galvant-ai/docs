Tell Dash what information you want to track. In this guide, you will create a dashboard, share it with your company, and have it emailed to you every week.

## Open a new dashboard

Open your company's Galvant app. Click **Dashboards** in the app sidebar, then **New dashboard**. Click **Ask Dash** to open the conversation panel.

## Review and save your dashboard

Look through the dashboard Dash proposes. Check that it shows the information you asked for, then click **Approve all** to save it.

If something needs to change, tell Dash what you want adjusted before approving.

## Share it with your company

1. Click **Share** in the dashboard header.
2. Choose **Everyone**, then click **Save changes**. This lets people in your company with Dashboards access view it.
3. Reopen **Share** and click **Copy link**. You can send this link to your teammates.

## Receive it by email every week

Set up a report to arrive in your own inbox every Monday at 9:00.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a new email schedule">
    Click **Schedule email** > **Scheduled sends** > **New schedule**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the recipient and weekly time">
    In **Recipients**, keep only your own email address. If you enter it, press **Enter** to add it.

    Set **Start** to next Monday, **Repeat** to **Weekly**, and **Every** to **1**. Under **On**, select only Monday. Set **Time** to **09:00** and check that **Timezone** is your timezone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the schedule">
    Check the **Occurs** summary, then click **Create schedule**. Your saved schedule now appears in the list. Each email will include an Excel report of the dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You now have a dashboard you can revisit, a link your teammates can open, and a weekly report scheduled for your inbox.
