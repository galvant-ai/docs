Describe the information you want to see, and Dash can turn it into a chart. You can also request a particular visualization or change one already on your dashboard.

On a dashboard you own, hover over a line or bar chart's header and click **Edit chart**, the sliders button, to change its presentation. Ask Dash to change the measures or grouping, or to change a pie or donut chart. Shared-dashboard viewers can explore the chart; use an owned copy to save edits.

## Combine measures in one chart

For example, ask Dash to show monthly sales beside gross margin, when those measures are available in your data:

<Prompt description="Show monthly sales as bars on the left axis and gross margin percentage as a line on the right axis. Label both axes with their units.">
  Show monthly sales as bars on the left axis and gross margin percentage as a line on the right axis. Label both axes with their units.
</Prompt>

Review the proposed chart and approve it. Read the sales bars against the currency axis and the margin line against the percentage axis.

<Info>
  **Screenshot placeholder: CHART-02**

To change how an existing measure is drawn, open **Edit chart** > **Series**, select the measure, and choose **Show as** > **Bar** or **Line**. A series is one measure drawn across the chart.

## Show components and proportions

Ask Dash for a breakdown when you want to see the parts of a total. For example: “Show monthly sales broken down by product group.”

On a chart containing bars, open **Edit chart** > **Bars**:

| Display | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| **Side by side** | Product-group amounts beside each other. |
| **Stacked** | The product-group amounts combined into each month's total. |
| **Stacked to 100%** | Each group's share of that month's total. |

<Info>
  **GIF placeholder: CHART-03**

  Switch one monthly product-group chart from Stacked to Stacked to 100%. Show how the axis and interpretation change from amounts to shares.
</Info>

Stack measures that make up the same total and use the same units. Dash can prepare the breakdown for you.

## Add targets and trends

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Show a target">
    To compare monthly sales with an agreed target:

    1. Open **Edit chart** > **Goal lines** > **Add goal line**.
    2. Enter the target **Value** and a **Label**, such as “Monthly target.”
    3. On a chart with two axes, select the sales axis.
    4. Choose its color and click **Add goal line**.

    <Info>
      **GIF placeholder: CHART-06**

    </Info>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Show the overall trend">
    Open **Edit chart** > **Series**, select a measure, and turn on **Trend line**. The fitted line summarizes the direction of the plotted points. It is not a forecast.

## Adjust labels, colors, and axes

In **Edit chart** > **Series**, select a measure to change its **Name**, **Color**, or **Data labels**. Use **Legend** to change where the legend appears.

<Info>
  **GIF placeholder: CHART-05**

  Give one series a clearer name and color. End with its matching legend entry and plotted values visible.
</Info>

Set the **Title** under **X axis** and **Y axis**, or **Left axis** and **Right axis**. Numeric-axis menus also offer **Format**. Label both axes when a chart combines different units.

<Info>
  **Screenshot placeholder: CHART-04**

  Show the right-axis menu on the combined sales/margin chart, with a clear percentage title and Percent format. Keep the resulting axis in view.
</Info>

These menu changes save directly when you can edit the chart. They are also available on editable line and bar cards in Chat.

<Accordion title="Additional presentation options">
  **Series** also contains line styles, points, curves, and area shading. Axis menus offer text sizes and **Limits** > **Auto** or **Manual**. Use a manual range carefully, because values outside it may be hidden.

  Line and bar charts support up to 10 series across both axes and up to five goal lines. You can edit an existing goal line with **Save goal line** or remove it with **Delete**.
</Accordion>
