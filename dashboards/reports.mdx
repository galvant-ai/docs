## Download Excel

Open the dashboard and click **Download Excel** in its header. Wait for the download to finish, then open the workbook to see its results and visualizations.

## Send a report now

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Send now">
    Click **Schedule email** in the dashboard header, then choose **Send now**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Address the report">
    Review **Recipients** and remove any addresses you do not want. Add the intended addresses, pressing **Enter** after each one. Review the **Subject** and **Message**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Check the recipients and click **Send now**. **Dashboard email queued** confirms the request was accepted for processing. Delivery happens afterward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Recipients receive the Excel file. They need [dashboard access](/dashboards/sharing) to open a live dashboard link included in the message.

## Schedule recurring emails

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a new schedule">
    Click **Schedule email** > **Scheduled sends** > **New schedule**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose recipients and timing">
    Review the **Recipients**, **Subject**, and **Message**. Set **Start**, **Repeat**, and **Every**. Where **On** is shown, choose the days or pattern. Set **Time** and **Timezone**.

    For example, a weekly team report can run every Monday at 09:00 in your team's timezone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check and save">
    Read the **Occurs** summary and check the dashboard's selected variables. Click **Create schedule**. The saved schedule appears in the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can create your own schedules for dashboards you own or can view through sharing.

## Change or stop a schedule

Open **Schedule email** > **Scheduled sends** and select one of your schedules. Change its recipients, message, or timing, then click **Save schedule**.

<Note>
  **Saving also replaces the schedule's variable selections with those currently selected on the dashboard.** Check the dashboard view before saving, even when you only want to edit the message.
</Note>

To stop future sends, select the schedule and click **Delete**. To finish on a particular date, set **End** to **On this day** and choose the end date instead.

## Which dates and variable values are used

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Download or send now" icon="sliders">
    The report uses the variable selections on the open dashboard.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Scheduled report" icon="calendar">
    The report uses the selections captured when you create or save that schedule.
  </Card>
</Columns>

For scheduled dates:

- **Dynamic** periods, such as Last 30 days, move with each send date in the schedule's timezone.
- **Static** dates stay fixed for every send.

Changing your open dashboard later does not update a saved schedule. Each scheduled report is generated at send time using the dashboard and the sender's available access.

A table's temporary search, sort, or column filters do not define the exported data. Use [dashboard variables](/dashboards/variables) for the report's selected scope.

## Check scheduled sends and delivery

Click **Scheduled sends** in the Dashboards sidebar to open the calendar. Use **Month** or **Week** to see upcoming occurrences and past sends. The legend distinguishes **You send** from **Sent to you**.

<Info>
  **Screenshot placeholder: REPORT-06**

  Show a weekly report on the Scheduled sends calendar with its date, time, and You send/Sent to you legend readable.
</Info>

If a delivery fails, the calendar shows **Delivery failed**. Your schedule's settings show **Last email failed** with its latest error. See [email troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting#an-export-or-scheduled-email-fails) for the next steps.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="More scheduling options">
    **Repeat** supports Daily, Weekly, Monthly, and Yearly. **Every** sets the interval, such as every two weeks. **On** offers weekdays or the monthly/yearly pattern associated with your start date. **End** can keep the schedule running or stop it on a date.

    A schedule belongs to its creator. Having access to a dashboard does not let you edit someone else's schedules.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Email options">
    You can add up to 25 recipients. **Include export time** adds the generation time to the message. Use **Copy dashboard link** to include the live view alongside the report.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Workbook contents and coverage">
    A multi-card export includes a Dashboard overview, card worksheets, and an Export Summary. A one-card dashboard exports on one worksheet.

    Each data worksheet has a 1,000,000-cell limit. Check the summary or affected worksheet for truncated results and card errors; some cards can succeed while another fails. Emailed reports also identify those issues.
