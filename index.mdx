## Meet Scout and Dash

Galvant has two assistants. Both work with the data connected to your company and available to your account.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Scout in Chat">
    Scout answers questions in a conversation. Type or dictate a message, answer its clarifying questions, and keep asking follow-ups in the same thread.

    - Ask about sales, finance, production, procurement, inventory, or quality.
    - Attach spreadsheets, PDFs, images, and STEP models and ask about what is inside them.
    - Get results as tables and charts you can expand, refresh, and download as Excel.
    - Open **Show logic** to see the steps, queries, and tables behind an answer.

    <Tip>
      New to Chat? Open **Chat** in the app sidebar, click **New chat**, and send one of the [starter questions](/chat/ask-questions#choose-your-first-question).
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Dash in Dashboards">
    Dash builds and maintains dashboards. Describe what you want to track and Dash chooses how to organize and visualize it. Nothing is saved until you click **Approve all**.

    - Combine tables, charts, numbers, and gauges on one dashboard.
    - Switch the period, customer, or location across several cards with dashboard variables.
    - Share a live dashboard with selected people or your whole company.
    - Download an Excel report, email it now, or schedule recurring delivery.

    <Tip>
      Build your first dashboard, share it, and schedule a weekly report in the [Dashboard quickstart](/dashboards/quickstart).
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Good to know

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Nothing changes without your approval">
    Dash shows a preview and a list of proposed changes. Click **Approve all** to save them or **Discard** to keep the dashboard as it is. Scout asks for approval before creating, updating, or deleting a workflow. Choose **Yes** in the approval card to allow it. A written reply requests a change; it does not approve one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What Scout can read from a file">
    Scout reads Excel, CSV, and TSV spreadsheets, PDFs, PNG, JPG, GIF, and WebP images, and STEP models. Each file can be up to 50 MB. Scout reads the first 1,000 rows of each sheet, so attach a smaller file if the records you need are further down. See [Work with files](/chat/files).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can see your work">
    Conversations belong to you. A new dashboard is private until you share it with selected people or with everyone in your company. Viewers can explore, refresh, and ask Dash questions, but need their own copy to change the saved setup. Pins for conversations, workflows, and dashboards are stored in your browser and do not change who can see them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How current the data is">
    Refreshing a card or dashboard runs the analysis again against the data available to Galvant. It does not trigger a sync from your company's source systems. A **Dynamic** date range moves with time, while a **Static** range stays fixed. If results look stale, check the selected variables first, then see [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting#data-does-not-look-current).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
