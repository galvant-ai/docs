Admin is where you manage who can use Galvant and what they can do. You see it in the left navigation when one of your roles grants Admin access. If you need it and do not see it, ask your current administrator.

Admin has two pages for everyday work. **Dashboard** lists the people in your company. **Roles** defines the permission bundles you assign to them. This guide covers the Dashboard. For roles, see [Roles and permissions](/admin/roles).

## Find a person

Open **Admin**. The Dashboard lists each person with their **Name**, **Email**, **Department**, **Type**, **Roles**, and **Last Active**. Use **Search users...** to find someone by name or email, or filter the **Type** column to show only people.

Click a person to open their account.

## Create a user

Users are created directly in Admin. There is no invitation email. Once the record exists and the person signs in with that email address, Galvant matches them to it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    On the Dashboard, click **New user**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their details">
    Under **User details**, enter the person's **First name**, **Last name**, and **Email**. Email is required and must match the address they sign in with. Add a **Job title** and choose a **Department** if you want them to appear in the list.

    Leave **Type** set to **Person**. The **Agent** type is for service accounts that Galvant sets up with you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose their roles">
    Under **Initial roles**, select one or more roles. A person receives the combined permissions of every role selected. You can change this later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save user**. The person now appears on the Dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit a person or change their roles

Open the person from the Dashboard. Under **User details**, update their name, job title, or department. The email address cannot be changed here.

Under **Assigned roles**, check or clear the roles this person should have. Then click **Save changes**. The new permissions apply the next time the person loads Galvant.

<Note>
  If your company's data is divided into portfolio companies, the person's page also shows **Portfolio companies**. Check each company whose data this person may use. Administrators can access every portfolio company.
</Note>

## Review a person's conversations and dashboards

A person's page also lists what they have built in Galvant.

- **Conversations** shows each conversation with its title, the number of user messages, and when it was created and updated. Click one to read the full exchange.
- **Dashboards** shows each dashboard they own, with its number of cards and when it was last updated.

When reading a conversation, click **Download** to save it as a Markdown file, or **Copy ID** if you need to reference it when contacting Galvant support.

<Tip>
  Reviewing conversations is a quick way to see which questions people ask most, and where a reusable workflow or a shared dashboard would save them time.
</Tip>
