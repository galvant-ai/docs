A conversation is a thread of messages with Scout. You can pick up an existing discussion, start a separate one, or save a repeatable task as a workflow.

## Choose a conversation, a new chat, or a workflow

| Choose | When to use it | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Continue an existing conversation** | Pick up where you left off. Scout can build on the discussion, results, and files in that thread. | “Break that result down by supplier.” |
| **Start a new chat** | Begin a separate discussion with fresh conversation context. | Discuss a production issue separately from a sales analysis. |
| **Create a reusable workflow** | Repeat an agreed task using saved instructions, with new inputs or fresh data. | Run the same supplier performance report each month. |

Keep working in the same conversation while you refine a task. Once you have a method you want to repeat, [save it as a reusable workflow](/chat/workflows). You can then run it again without explaining the method each time.

Starting a new chat leaves your previous conversations in history. It gives you a separate conversation context; your connected-data access stays the same.

## Pin and unpin conversations

Hover over a conversation to reveal its pin control. Click **Pin** to move it into **Pinned**. Click **Unpin** to return it to the recent list.

<Note>
  Conversation pins are stored in your browser. Pinning does not share a conversation or change who can access it.
</Note>

## Rename a conversation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the conversation menu">
    Hover over the conversation and open its three-dot menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the title">
    Choose **Rename** and enter a title you will recognize later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the title">
    Press **Enter** to save. Press **Escape** to cancel the edit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Delete a conversation

Open the conversation's three-dot menu and choose **Delete**. This removes the conversation from your history. If you delete the conversation you are viewing, Chat opens a new chat.

<Warning>
  Delete a conversation only when you no longer need it. Choose **Rename** or **Pin** when your goal is to organize your history.
</Warning>

For repeating an analysis without keeping all its discussion in the same thread, see [Reusable workflows](/chat/workflows).
