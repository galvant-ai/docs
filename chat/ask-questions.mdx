Scout is your personal assistant for your company's data. Type or dictate a message and Scout will look through your connected systems, ask if something is unclear, and bring back an answer.

## Start a chat

Click **Chat** in the app sidebar, then **New chat**. Type your message and press **Enter** to send. Press **Shift+Enter** to start a new line in the message box.

## Dictate a message

You can speak your message in English and turn it into text before sending.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start recording">
    Click the microphone button in the message box. Its tooltip is **Dictate a message**. Allow microphone access if your browser asks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop and transcribe">
    Speak your message, then click **Stop and transcribe**, the square stop button. Wait for the transcription to appear in the message box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and send">
    Check the text, especially names, dates, and numbers. Edit it if needed, then send your message.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Dictate a message, review the transcription, and send it.">
  <video autoPlay muted loop playsInline src="/assets/clips/chat_talk.mp4" />
</Frame>

Recordings have a 10-minute limit. Click **Discard recording**, the X button, to discard one. If transcription fails and **Retry** appears, use it to try again. For microphone errors, see [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting#dictation-does-not-work).

## Respond to Scout's questions

Scout may ask you to choose a time period, clarify a term, or decide how to continue. The **Question** card appears near the message box while Scout waits for your response.

- Click one of the offered answers, or write your own response.
- Selecting an offered answer advances to the next question or submits the final response. For a written answer, click **Next**, or **Send** on the last question.
- Use the back arrow to revisit an earlier question before submitting.
- Click **Skip** if you do not want to provide the requested answer.

When Scout is waiting on a question, respond through that card to continue the request. Starting another conversation does not answer the question in this one.

## Ask follow-up questions

Send related questions in the same conversation so Scout can use its earlier messages, results, and attachments.

Scout may suggest a follow-up in the empty message box after finishing an answer. With the message box focused:

1. Press **Tab** to accept the suggestion into your draft.
2. Edit the text if you want to change it.
3. Press **Enter** to send.

Press **Escape** to dismiss a suggestion. You can also type your own message. A suggestion is optional and does not appear after every answer.
