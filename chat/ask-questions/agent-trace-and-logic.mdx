Scout's agent trace shows its work as a request runs. **Show logic** explains the thought process and steps Scout took to reach its answer.

## Open the agent trace

The trace includes data searches, table inspections, queries, and tool results. The steps depend on the request.

While Scout is working, the trace opens under **Working**. Once the final answer starts, it collapses under **Worked for** followed by the elapsed time. Click that control to reopen it.

Expand a tool call to inspect its available details. For example, a **Queried database** step can show the **SQL** and the number of **Rows** returned. Tool calls without additional details do not have an expandable detail view.

## See how Scout reached an answer

Click **Show logic** beneath the answer to follow Scout's thought process and the steps that led to the result. For answers using connected data, **Tables used** identifies the sources involved.

If Scout builds on an earlier result in the conversation, Show logic can refer back to that work.

Result cards also have **Show logic** in their headers. Hover over the card header to reveal its controls, then open **Show logic** to see the steps behind that table or chart.
