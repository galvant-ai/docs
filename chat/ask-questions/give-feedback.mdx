Use **Give feedback** to report a problem with a specific Scout answer. The feedback includes its conversation and message, so the response can be identified.

## Submit feedback

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the feedback form">
    Click **Give feedback** beneath the answer you want to report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the issue">
    Enter what happened and what you expected. Include the relevant date range, measure, or example record when it helps identify the problem.

    For example: “I asked for last month's sales, but the answer uses this month's dates.”
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the feedback">
    Click **Submit**. The form closes when submission succeeds. Click **Cancel** if you do not want to submit it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Ask Scout to revise the answer

Submitting feedback does not revise the answer or continue the analysis. Send a message in the same conversation when you want Scout to correct the result.

To review Scout's steps before reporting an issue, see [Agent trace and Show logic](/chat/ask-questions/agent-trace-and-logic). For access, recording, or delivery problems, see [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting).
